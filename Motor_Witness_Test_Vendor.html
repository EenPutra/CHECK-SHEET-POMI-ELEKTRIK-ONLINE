📖 Panduan Interpretasi Partial Discharge

QM = nilai peak magnitude; makin besar QM makin besar void di isolasi (udara terjebak / contaminant).

Symptom umum:

1. No polarity predominance (+QM ≈ −QM) → internal void, thermal deterioration, poor impregnation.

2. Positive polarity predominance (+QM > −QM) → discharge di permukaan coil, loose winding, deterioration voltage stress coating.

3. Negative polarity predominance (+QM < −QM) → discharge dekat konduktor tembaga, load cycling, overheating void dekat tembaga (monitoring saja).

4. Phase shifted (PD bergerak ±30/60°) → indikasi phase-to-phase PD.

PD paling sering muncul di sudut ~45° & ~225° → phase-to-ground discharge antara winding dan core.
